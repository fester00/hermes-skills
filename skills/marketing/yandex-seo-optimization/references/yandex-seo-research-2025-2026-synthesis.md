# Yandex SEO 2025–2026 research synthesis

Condensed findings from 15+ sources (official Yandex docs + SEOnews/PR-CY/ConvertMonster articles, 2025-06).

## Big picture

- Yandex ranking is combinatorial: YATI neural retrieval + MatrixNet/CatBoost + behavioural/commercial/trust signals.
- No single silver bullet; consistency across all groups matters.
- AI search (YandexGPT / Алиса / generative answers) is changing CTR and creating GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

## 1. Commercial factors (critical for shops/services)

- Full contact info, address, hours, map.
- Reviews on site + Yandex Maps / Market / external platforms.
- Actual prices, stock, assortment.
- Delivery, payment, warranty, return policies.
- Legal info: INN, OGRN, offer, privacy policy.
- Yandex Business / Maps presence.
- **Yandex Commerce Protocol (YCP)** for structured product feeds and Alice integration.

## 2. Content factors

- Match user intent precisely.
- E-E-A-T-like expertise, especially YMYL (medicine, finance, law).
- Original, complete, well-structured content (H1-H6, lists, tables, FAQ).
- Freshness for trending topics.
- AI-generated content allowed only after human editing.
- GEO: clear direct answers, FAQ markup, lists/tables, HowTo schema.

## 3. Technical factors

- Mobile-first adaptive design.
- HTTPS, correct 200/404, no Soft 404.
- robots.txt, sitemap.xml, canonical, noindex for utility pages.
- Clean URLs, reasonable depth, HTML links (not JS-only).
- Pages >10 MB not indexed.
- Correct Last-Modified headers.
- WebP/AVIF images, reasonable dimensions.
- Core Web Vitals matter for UX and indirectly for ranking.

## 4. Behavioural factors

- CTR from SERP.
- Dwell time and depth.
- Pogo-sticking (return to SERP quickly) is negative.
- Conversions for commercial queries.
- Yandex Metrica with goals.

## 5. Links

- Quality > quantity.
- Natural profile, relevant donors.
- Sponsored / UGC rel attributes.
- Spam/paid links risk penalties.

## 6. Local / trust / ecosystem

- Yandex Business / Maps for local.
- Site age, no sanctions, stable uptime, no malware.
- Integration: Webmaster, Metrica, Business, Market, Zen.

## Title / Description limits used in this research

| Element | Recommended length |
|---|---|
| `title` | ≤70 characters |
| `meta description` | ≤160 characters |

## Key contradictions between sources

- Behavioural factors: SEO experts say very important; Yandex says one of many signals.
- Text length: "short and to the point" vs "top pages are longer" — actual factor is completeness, not length.
- Links: work fast in low-competition niches but weak without content/commercial/technical base.
- AI content: allowed but must be useful and edited.
- YandexGPT: may lower CTR for informational queries; limited effect on commercial queries.
